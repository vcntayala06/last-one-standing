# Stage 6.14 player Host-style review

Players explicitly choose Neutral, Masculine, or Feminine. Neutral is the default. Style changes Host wording only; it never changes questions, scoring, difficulty, timing, or answer matching. No style is inferred from a name.

## Editorial checks

- Production includes at least 20 Work-safe personalized choices across the three banks above.
- Production includes at least 20 humorous personalized/reaction choices in the broader Host bank.
- Spanish-containing lines remain subject to the separate pronunciation sheet. Recurring slang `compa`, `perro`, and `órale` is gated from automatic selection until listening approval.
- Appearance-based compliments and experimental fragments such as “gr” are excluded.
