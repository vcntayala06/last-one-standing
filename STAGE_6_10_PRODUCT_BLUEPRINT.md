# Stage 6.10 Playtest Audit and Deferred Product Blueprint

## Difficulty audit

The visible-difficulty mapping was contributing to the live Easy complaint. Adult Easy previously selected both `easy` and `kids-hard` records. Medium also admitted Hard, and Hard admitted Savage. Stage 6.10 narrows the mapping:

- Kids: Kids Easy, Kids Medium, Kids Hard
- Easy: Kids Easy, Kids Medium, Easy
- Medium: Easy, Medium
- Hard: Medium, Hard
- Savage: Hard, Savage

The selection index, edition filtering, used-question tracking, and Work 70/30 selection are otherwise operating correctly.

Content calibration still needs a later editorial pass. The clearest Easy candidates to review are Batch 3 Music IDs `los-b3-music-052` through `los-b3-music-090`, especially clues involving Rufus, Ana Gabriel, Rocío Dúrcal, Grupo Límite, Paquita la del Barrio, Ramón Ayala, and specific Regional Mexican songs or nicknames. These can be excellent questions, but several are better suited to Medium for a broad adult audience. No question metadata was changed in Stage 6.10.

## Recommended Quick Setup

Use one responsive setup stage with progressive sections rather than a long chain of full-screen clicks:

1. Game choice: Original, Work Edition, or Solo
2. Players and names when multiplayer applies
3. Difficulty
4. Answer time and game length
5. Compact Voice and Volume controls
6. One clear Start action

Work-only industry information can appear conditionally. Solo should hide multiplayer player entry. Preserve the existing visual identity and canonical actions so touch and voice continue to share behavior.

## Kids game blueprint

Kids should eventually become a choice under Choose Your Game rather than remaining an adult difficulty option. Its background metadata should continue to support approximately grades 4–7, with age-appropriate educational and entertainment content. A future design can expose a simple grade or age band after Kids is selected without adding complexity to adult setup.

## Crowd Awareness blueprint

Crowd Awareness is a future optional experiment only. It may occasionally respond to laughter, celebration, teasing, or friendly competition, with strict guardrails:

- Never listen or react during a question or answer window
- Never affect answer recognition
- Never repeat sensitive or offensive speech
- Never escalate insults
- Never comment constantly
- Keep reactions short, playful, and optional
- Process only transient session context; store no crowd transcript

No Crowd Awareness behavior is implemented in Stage 6.10.
